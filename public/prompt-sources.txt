JINX KISS - PROMPT SOURCES AND LICENSE NOTICE
Reviewed 2026-10-25

This expansion combines two licensed question reproductions with original
questions informed by established party-game formats. Commercial card decks
were not copied. References do not imply endorsement or affiliation.
Individual new prompts have not been measured for popularity.

parabol: Parabol Icebreakers
https://github.com/ParabolInc/icebreakers/blob/35b3f1579852e1e1e535983ab7a503b6e0e44550/lib/api.ts
Two licensed questions; other entries are original variations on conversation starters.

wnrs: We're Not Really Strangers
https://www.werenotreallystrangers.com/pages/core-game
Original questions about perception, connection, and relationship reflection.

cut: Truth or Drink — Cut
https://www.cut.com/truth-or-drink-party-games
Original personal questions with an answer-or-pass response.

voting: The Voting Game — Dyce Games
https://dycegames.com/collections/the-voting-game
Original player nominations based on revealing situations.

poison: Pick Your Poison — Dyce Games
https://dycegames.com/collections/pick-your-poison
Original paired dilemmas with comparable costs.

opinions: Bad Opinions — Dyce Games
https://dycegames.com/collections/bad-opinions
Original statements suited to agree/disagree debate.

seat: Hot Seat — Dyce Games
https://dycegames.com/collections/hot-seat
Original questions about one spotlighted player; no impersonation rule added.

nhie: Never Have I Ever — PsyCat Games
https://psycatgames.com/magazine/party-games/never-ever/
Original admissions drawn from familiar confession-game situations.

tod: Truth or Dare — PsyCat Games
https://psycatgames.com/magazine/party-games/truth-or-dare/
Original truth questions and playable challenges, separated by type.

flags: Red Flags — Gameology retailer overview
https://www.gameology.com.au/products/red-flags
Original perk-versus-dealbreaker scenarios adapted to flag voting.

roles: Role Models — Jackbox Games
https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/role-models
Original group-role predictions adapted to single-player nominations.
